01About

I'm a mechanical engineer at Winomechanic Pvt. Ltd., a Leitwind Group (Italy) subsidiary, working on wind turbine component manufacturing in Gummidipoondi, Tamil Nadu.

"You don't design a good fixture from a drawing alone — you design it from having stood at the machine."

My path so far has run across the machine shop, coil shop, and non-destructive testing — I'm certified at Level 1 in Visual, Magnetic Particle, and Liquid Penetrant testing. That gave me a working knowledge of tolerances, failure modes, and manufacturability that most design-only engineers pick up much later.

I'm now building toward design engineering roles — using SolidWorks and AutoCAD to design and modify fixtures, and applying that same shop-floor thinking to how a part should be drawn in the first place.
